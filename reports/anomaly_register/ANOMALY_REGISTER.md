# CAG–ICSSR State Social Sector Spending: Anomaly Register & Audit Follow-up Catalog

## 1. Overview & Anomaly Taxonomy
The Anomaly Register identifies state-year observations where social sector expenditure, sub-sector allocations, or budget execution metrics departed significantly from empirical expectations. The computational framework leverages three complementary detection paradigms:
1. **Statistical (Robust Z-Score via Median Absolute Deviation - MAD):** Detects sudden year-on-year accelerations or contractions ($|Z_{rob}| \ge 2.8$).
2. **Multivariate Machine Learning (Isolation Forest & Local Outlier Factor):** Evaluates joint anomalies across expenditure, fiscal effort, and budget variances.
3. **Budget Execution & Grant Variance:** Flags execution ratios $BE\_ACT < 0.82$ (severe shortfall) or $> 1.08$ (unanticipated surge), and grant savings/excess $> 15\%$.

Each flagged observation is triangulated against historical macroeconomic and institutional records, classifying it into one of three operational categories:
- **Category A (Event-Explained):** Known external shocks (Disasters, Pandemics, Reorganisation Acts).
- **Category B (Budget Execution Bottlenecks):** Chronic underspending or unratified supplementary grants.
- **Category C (Audit Priority Flags):** Departures without clear administrative explanations, earmarked for field audit scrutiny.

## 3. Methodological Protocol for Audit Follow-up
For observations categorized under **Category C**, state audit offices should execute the following 4-step verification protocol:
1. **Treasury Voucher Examination:** Sample treasury vouchers for the final quarter (January–March) to inspect whether outlays represent actual ground disbursements or parking of funds in Personal Deposit (PD) accounts.
2. **Detailed Appropriation Audit:** Cross-verify Grant numbers for Education (Grant 15/20), Health, and Social Security to detect unratified re-appropriations in violation of Article 205.
3. **Centrally Sponsored Scheme (CSS) SNA Audit:** Reconcile unspent balances in State Nodal Agency accounts with Public Financial Management System (PFMS) records.
4. **Outcome Validation:** Compare high-spending years against physical outcomes (UDISE+ pupil-teacher ratios, NFHS immunization coverage) to detect input-output leakage.
